How to move

Three ways around the room.

  • ExploreDrag to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Right-drag to pan.
  • WalkWASD or arrow keys to move. Drag to look. On touch: left thumb moves, right thumb looks.
  • DollhouseZooms out to the whole scan, like a model. Tap again to fly back.

Heads up: exterior views of an interior scan can look rough. That's the scan, not the viewer.
